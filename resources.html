Legal & Professional Resources

Guidance for employment discrimination and security incidents

Employment Discrimination & Wage Theft

Your Situation May Involve Multiple Violations:
  • Wage Theft: Unauthorized salary reductions without consent
  • Visa Discrimination: Mistreatment based on work permit expiration (employers have duties)
  • Retaliation: Negative treatment for asserting rights
  • Contract Coercion: Forced signature under duress

Government Labor Agencies

  • US Department of Labor: wage-hour.dol.gov
  • EEOC (Employment): eeoc.gov - Discrimination complaints
  • State Labor Board: Search "[YOUR_STATE] Department of Labor"
  • Wage Claims: Check your state labor board website

Legal Help

  • Employment Lawyers: Many offer free consultations
  • Legal Aid: lawhelp.org - Free legal services
  • Bar Associations: Referral services for lawyers
  • Class Action Info: Check if part of group wage theft

Documentation Templates

  • Wage Demand Letter: Formal request for unpaid wages
  • Complaint Template: File with labor agency
  • Timeline Document: Chronological incident list
  • Witness Statement: Get statements from coworkers
Important Timeline: Wage theft claims often have statute of limitations (2-4 years typically). Act quickly by documenting and filing complaints.

Documentation Checklist

  • ☐ All pay stubs since March 20 (showing salary reductions)
  • ☐ Original employment contract/offer letter
  • ☐ Any new contract you were asked to sign
  • ☐ Emails about salary changes or work permit
  • ☐ HR communications and denial of benefits
  • ☐ Performance reviews showing your work value
  • ☐ Comparison of wages to other employees (same role)
  • ☐ Text/email messages from HR or management
  • ☐ Witness contact information
  • ☐ Calendar with dates of key conversations
  • ☐ Work visa/permit documentation

Key Messages NOT to Sign

DO NOT SIGN anything without lawyer review:
  • New contracts changing your rights
  • Settlement agreements
  • NDA that covers illegal activities
  • Documents acknowledging wage cuts as legitimate
  • Anything without reading/understanding it fully

Cybersecurity & Unauthorized Access

Critical: Do NOT attempt DIY forensics or "hacking back"

This is illegal and destroys evidence for prosecution. Professional forensics firms must handle investigation.

Law Enforcement

  • FBI IC3: ic3.gov (Internet Crime)
  • Local Police: File report at precinct
  • FBI Field Office: fbi.gov/contact
  • Secret Service: USSS.gov (if financial fraud)

Forensics & Investigation

  • Certified Forensics: Look for GCIH, CFCE certifications
  • IACIS Members: iacis.com
  • EnCase Certified: Reputable digital forensics
  • Expert Witness: Court-testifiable investigations

ISP & Abuse Reporting

  • ISP Abuse Department: Report IPs to your provider
  • Hosting Providers: Report to server hosts
  • Domain Registrars: Report suspicious domains
  • ABUSEIPDB: abuse.net - IP reputation

Immediate Security Actions

  • ☐ Document suspicious activity with exact timestamps
  • ☐ Screenshot unusual system behavior
  • ☐ Note IP addresses from logs/firewalls
  • ☐ Preserve system logs (don't delete)
  • ☐ Take photos of unusual network activity
  • ☐ Save email headers (contain IP info)
  • ☐ Check router logs for unauthorized access
  • ☐ List all devices that could be compromised
  • ☐ Note dates when issues first noticed
  • ☐ Engage forensics professional BEFORE cleaning
Why Professional Forensics Matter:
  • Evidence is admissible in court
  • Proper chain of custody
  • Expert testimony available
  • Detailed technical analysis
  • Timeline documentation

What NOT to Do

  • ❌ Attempt to "hack back" or retaliate
  • ❌ Access other people's accounts without authorization
  • ❌ Delete logs or evidence
  • ❌ Run forensics tools yourself (destroys evidence)
  • ❌ Post details publicly on social media
  • ❌ Contact the attacker directly
  • ❌ Attempt DIY malware removal
  • ❌ Ignore the problem hoping it stops

If Both Issues Are Related (Workplace Retaliation + Hacking)

If your employer or HR is involved in the hacking:
  • This becomes serious criminal/civil matter
  • Document everything about the connection
  • Report to law enforcement immediately
  • Consult both employment AND criminal lawyer
  • Preserve all evidence
  • Consider EEOC complaint (hacking as harassment)
Issue Type Immediate Action Contact Timeline
Wage Theft File Department of Labor complaint DOL Wage-Hour Division Within 2-4 years (varies by state)
Unauthorized Access File police report + engage forensics Local Police, FBI IC3 ASAP - evidence degrades
Visa Discrimination Consult immigration + employment lawyer Immigration lawyer, EEOC Before signing anything
Contract Coercion Consult employment lawyer Employment lawyer Before signing

  2. ☐ Employment Lawyer: For wage theft & contract review
  3. ☐ Cybersecurity Forensics Firm: For hacking investigation
  4. ☐ Law Enforcement: For criminal hacking report
  5. ☐ Accountant/Tax Professional: For wage documentation

Important Reminders

You have rights:
  • Right to fair wages for all work
  • Right to safe working conditions
  • Right to non-discrimination
  • Right to freedom from harassment
  • Right to privacy and security
Protection exists even if your visa/permit expired. Many countries protect workers regardless of immigration status. Consult a lawyer.
